#### Design Specificity Verdict

Suspension telemetry tracks damping clicks from fully closed, tokens, and dual-unit pressures; component mounting reflects physical slots with historical wear continuity across frames; and Quick Replace directly replicates grabbing a spare chain from a workshop peg. #### Overall Impression
BikeVault possesses rare mechanical literacy and exceptional domain depth for cyclists and mechanics. Its primary ergonomic bottleneck is form density: key workflows (like bike onboarding) rely on monolithic, scrolling modal dialogs rather than structured progressive disclosure.

#### What's Working
1. **Quick Replace Component Pattern (QuickReplaceModal.tsx):** Flawlessly captures workshop reality when swapping worn parts for fresh ones from storage, preserving the old part's lifetime wear while mounting the replacement at the bike's current odometer.
2. **Suspension & Cockpit Telemetry Fidelity (SetupClient.tsx):** Accurately tracks LSC/HSC/LSR/HSR clicks from fully closed, volume tokens, and tire pressures linked to compound, casing, and inserts.
3. **Defensive Telemetry Guardrails (UpdateOdometerModal.tsx):** Real-time delta previews (+35.2 km • +2h 15m) eliminate arithmetic errors, while decrement protection stops accidental typos.

#### Priority Issues
- **[P0] Missing Ad-Hoc Expense Entry in Global Finances**
  - **Why it matters:** Cyclists frequently purchase workshop supplies (sealant, chain wax, brake fluid, tools) or pay race fees not bound to a bike purchase. Without ad-hoc entry, financial records and TCO are incomplete.
  - **Fix:** Build and mount AddTransactionModal.tsx in Global Finances with category selection, amount, date, optional bike/component association, and receipt/note fields.
  - **Suggested command:** /impeccable shape

- **[P1] Monolithic Scroll Fatigue in BikeModal**
  - **Why it matters:** Over 20 fields presented in a single vertical scroll container causing cognitive overload and high onboarding friction on small laptops and mobile screens.
  - **Fix:** Chunk into a 3-step progressive wizard or tabbed layout (1. Identity & Photo, 2. Specs & Geometry, 3. Acquisition & Initial Mileage).
  - **Suggested command:** /impeccable layout

- **[P2] Triple Code Duplication of Service Event Recording**
  - **Why it matters:** RecordServiceEventModal logic is duplicated inline across BikeServiceClient, ComponentDetailClient, and MaintenanceClient, multiplying maintenance costs and causing UI drift.
  - **Fix:** Consolidate into a single shared RecordServiceEventModal component with polymorphic bike/component selection.
  - **Suggested command:** /impeccable distill

- **[P3] Lack of Contextual Help and Guided Empty States**
  - **Why it matters:** Novice riders are intimidated by technical suspension clickers (HSC/LSC, sag) with zero guidance, leaving setup tabs empty.
  - **Fix:** Add micro-popover tooltips on damping dials and add guided empty states with recommended baseline configurations.
  - **Suggested command:** /impeccable onboard

#### Persona Red Flags

- **Jordan (First-Timer / Casual Cyclist):** Overwhelmed by BikeModal's technical dropdowns (Suspension Type, Drive Type) and confused by unexplained LSC/HSC abbreviations.
- **Sam (Accessibility-Dependent / Keyboard & Screen Reader):** Long modal dialogs with nested scroll containers (max-h-[82vh]) and flat input lists without <fieldset> / <legend> groupings make linear keyboard navigation exhausting.
