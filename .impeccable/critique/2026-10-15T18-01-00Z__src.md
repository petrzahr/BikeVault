## 2. Assessment A: Design Director Review

### Design Specificity Verdict: High-Precision Cycling Workshop Tool (Definitive Transition Completed)
The previous iteration sat awkwardly between a generic SaaS table dashboard and an enthusiast's logbook. With the implementation of the **3-step progressive BikeModal**, the **unified RecordServiceEventModal**, and **AddTransactionModal**, the application has decisively crossed the threshold into a **purpose-built precision cycling workshop tool**.

The application demonstrates authentic domain mastery:
- **Suspension Calibration Ergonomics**: Suspension dials are measured in clicks from fully closed (clockwise), air pressures in PSI, sag in %, and volume tokens with exact counts (`SetupClient.tsx`).
- **Component Lifecycle & Disposition**: Swapping a worn chain or tire is not a generic database update; it honors real garage mechanics via `QuickReplaceModal.tsx`, automatically archiving the retired component with accumulated kilometers while installing a compatible spare from the warehouse.
- **Duality of DIY vs. Pro Workshop**: Maintenance recording distinguishes between home wrenching (*„Svépomocí”*) and commercial workshops (*„Dílna”*), separating parts costs from labor fees and instantly synchronizing with TCO accounting (`RecordServiceEventModal.tsx`).

### Nielsen's 10 Heuristics Detailed Breakdown

1. **Visibility of System Status: 3.6 / 4**
   Real-time calculation of odometer differences in `UpdateOdometerModal.tsx`; dynamic summation of Parts + Labor cost with immediate TCO ledger notice in `RecordServiceEventModal.tsx`; clear step numbering (1, 2, 3) in `BikeModal.tsx`.
2. **Match Between System and Real World: 4.0 / 4**
   Exceptional cycling fidelity. Distinguishes DIY vs. bike shop labor, casing/compounds for tires, suspension damping clicks, and realistic service templates (50h lower leg service, 200h damper overhaul). Czech workshop vernacular is authentic and natural.
3. **User Control and Freedom: 3.5 / 4**
   High freedom: non-linear tab jumping in `BikeModal.tsx` allows saving immediately from Tab 1 without forcing users through all 3 tabs; reversible component transfers. *Minor gap:* Bike-level finances lack direct access to `AddTransactionModal.tsx`.
4. **Consistency and Standards: 3.7 / 4**
   The deduplication of `RecordServiceEventModal` eliminated three competing modal implementations. Metric ribbons (`grid grid-cols-3 divide-x`) are strictly unified across Global Finances, Bike Finances, and Statistics. Button hierarchies adhere to shared tokens in `ui.ts`.

   `UpdateOdometerModal.tsx` detects negative delta regressions and blocks them unless an explicit correction toggle is checked. `DeleteBikeModal.tsx` actively asks how to handle installed components (*Vrátit do skladu* vs. *Trvale smazat*), preventing accidental data loss.
6. **Recognition Rather Than Recall: 3.8 / 4**

8. **Aesthetic and Minimalist Design: 3.7 / 4**
   Calibrated Slate/Navy palette with disciplined semantic accents (emerald for active/income, amber for upcoming service, rose for overdue/expense). Chunking `BikeModal` from 20 fields into 3 paced tabs reduced visual noise significantly.

## 3. Form Ergonomics & Modal Patterns Analysis

### A. `BikeModal.tsx` (3-Step Progressive Wizard)
- **The Transformation:** Previously an overwhelming single-scroll dialog containing over 18 mixed-domain input fields (brand, geometry, suspension, purchase price, initial odometer, notes, photos).
- **The New Architecture:** Segmented into three distinct mental models:
  1. **Tab 1: Identita & Foto**: Core recognition fields (Name, Brand, Model, Year, Category, Discipline, Photo Upload/URL, Notes).
  2. **Tab 2: Specifikace**: Mechanical configuration (Suspension type, Drive type, Frame size, Serial number, Weight kg).
  3. **Tab 3: Pořízení & Nájezd**: Historical telemetry & financial anchor (Status, Purchase Date, Price, Initial Km & Hours).
- **Ergonomic Triumphs:**
  - **Early Exit Capability**: Power users adding a quick bike stub do not need to click through to Tab 3. A secondary *„Uložit”* button remains accessible on Tabs 1 and 2 once required fields are valid.
  - **Paced Input Validation**: Transitioning from Tab 1 to Tab 2 via *„Další: Specifikace”* validates mandatory name/manufacturer/model before advancing, eliminating surprise submit failures at the end.
  - **Accessible Segmented Control**: Numbered chips (1, 2, 3) indicate progress while remaining clickable for non-linear jumping.

### B. `AddTransactionModal.tsx` (Ad-hoc Expenses & Incomes)
- **Ergonomics & Layout:** Compact (`max-w-md`), single-column hierarchy designed for rapid data entry.
- **Mental Model Alignment:** Top-level segmented toggle between **Výdaj** (Rose) and **Příjem** (Emerald). Selecting either mode immediately re-contextualizes the category select options:
  - *Výdaj:* Consumables (sealant, chain lube), Accessories, Upgrades, Service parts, Service labor, Other.
  - *Příjem:* Component sale, Bike sale, Refund/Warranty, Other.
- **Financial Integration:** Connects seamlessly with `VaultContext`, allowing optional linking to a specific bike in the fleet.

### C. `RecordServiceEventModal.tsx` (Unified Maintenance Logger)
- **Deduplication Victory:** Eliminated three divergent modal instances across `MaintenanceClient.tsx`, `BikeServiceClient.tsx`, and `ComponentDetailClient.tsx`.
- **Context Awareness:**
  - When opened with `fixedBikeId` (from bike service tab), the bike selector is cleanly hidden and locked.
  - When opened from a specific due schedule (`initialSchedule`), title dynamically shifts to *„Zapsat provedení servisu”*, automatically binding the interval reset.
  - When opened ad-hoc, title becomes *„Zapsat servisní úkon bez plánu”*.
- **Live Calculation Ergonomics:** Separate *Cena dílů* and *Cena práce* inputs dynamically compute the total with a live chip: `Celkem: 1 250 Kč`, explicitly alerting the user that a financial transaction will be logged to the bike's ledger automatically.
